GED & Arizona HSE Diploma Pathway FinderThree approved ways to finish — and the free classes in Maricopa County

Most people are told to “go get your GED.” In Arizona that advice is incomplete, and the gap costs people months. This page explains what Arizona actually awards, helps you pick which of the three pathways fits what you have already done, and gives you the free programs to call.

The part nobody mentions: in Arizona, the GED is not the diploma.

The GED® is a set of tests owned by a private company. The credential is the Arizona High School Equivalency Diploma, awarded by the Arizona Department of Education. Passing the GED tests is only one of three approved ways to earn it.

All three ways also require passing the Arizona Civics Test. People pass all four GED subjects, wait for a diploma that never arrives, and only then find out about the civics requirement. That is the single most common way this process stalls.

    Every pathway ends at the same gate: the Arizona Civics Test

    Whichever route you take, Arizona will not issue the diploma until you pass this. It is worth doing early rather than last, because it is the one piece that surprises people.

    • You need 70 out of 100. The passing score rose from 60 to 70 on January 1, 2026. Anything you read that still says 60 is out of date.
    • Retakes are unlimited, with no waiting period between attempts.
    • A passing score from middle school or high school can count. If you took civics before you left school, ask the Arizona Department of Education before you pay to sit it again.
    • You register for it on GED.com, even if you are using the College Credit or HSE PLUS pathway. It can be taken online with a proctor or at a testing center.
    • On the College Credit Pathway you must pass civics before submitting any paperwork to the state.

    Questions about civics or your pathway: Arizona Department of Education, Adult Education Services — 602-258-2410 or AdultEd@azed.gov.

    What the GED testing route actually costs in Arizona

    This applies only to the GED Testing Pathway. The College Credit and HSE PLUS pathways have different costs, and free adult education classes are free on every pathway.

    Arizona GED test fees, verified September 21, 2026
    One subject, test center or online$41
    All four subjects$164
    Retake at a test center$15
    Retake online$41
    Free classes to prepare$0

    You do not have to pay for all four at once. Subjects are scheduled and paid for one at a time, so $41 is the real entry price, not $164.

      Questions people actually ask

      Is a GED worth less than a real diploma?

      Employers and Arizona public universities and community colleges accept the Arizona HSE Diploma. Some selective private colleges and a few military occupational specialties weight a traditional diploma more heavily. If a standard diploma matters to you and you are 18 or older, Phoenix has a route to one: The Excel Center is a tuition-free public charter high school for adults that awards an actual high school diploma, with free childcare and transportation help — 602-535-4401, 4837 E. McDowell Rd., Phoenix. It takes longer than testing, and for many people that trade is worth it.

      I took the GED years ago. Do my old scores still count?

      The GED test was fully redesigned in 2014, and scores from before that are generally no longer usable toward the current credential. Scores from the 2014 test series onward are typically still on file in your GED.com account. Do not assume either way — log in at GED.com, or call the Arizona Department of Education at 602-258-2410, before you pay to retake anything.

      Can I combine college credits with GED scores?

      Not on the College Credit Pathway — Arizona is explicit that college credits cannot be combined with GED passing scores or high school credits on that route. The HSE PLUS Career Readiness Pathway is the one built for combining: it lets high school credits, college credits, GED subjects passed, adult education coursework, work certificates, and employment all count toward a single point total. If you have a scattered mix of partial progress, HSE PLUS is usually the pathway worth asking about.

      Do I have to be an Arizona resident?

      No. Arizona has no residency requirement for testing. But you must take your last test in Arizona to receive the Arizona diploma, so if you are moving mid-process, finish here or check the rules in the state you are moving to first.

      Can I test in Spanish?

      Yes. The GED test is offered in English and Spanish in Arizona, and since August 2019 you can combine the two languages across subjects to earn the credential. Several Maricopa County adult education programs teach in Spanish; ask when you call.

      Do I need to take a class first?

      No. Arizona does not require classroom preparation before testing. But the classes are free, they are run by state-funded programs, and they are the only door to the HSE PLUS pathway — so there is very little reason not to at least call one.

      Verify before you go. Phone numbers, class schedules, and fees change.
