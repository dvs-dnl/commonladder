Learn about homelessness

Common belief

“Most people experiencing homelessness are chronically homeless — living on the street for years.”

Tap to see what the data says
What the data says

That picture is roughly a quarter of the population in any given year. The rest are families, kids, workers, veterans, and people you never see.

Pathway 02: Who experiences it
Common belief

“Homelessness is caused by addiction, mental illness, and personal failure.”

Tap to see what the data says
What the data says

Those rank third or fourth on the list. The dominant variable is the housing market itself — cost and supply, not character.

Pathway 01: What causes it
Common belief

“Nobody actually knows how to fix homelessness.”

Tap to see what the data says
What the data says

We have decades of evidence on what works — housing, immediately, without preconditions. The gap is funding and political will, not knowledge.
