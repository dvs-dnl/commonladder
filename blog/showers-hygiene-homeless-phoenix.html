Where to Shower, Wash Clothes, and Get Clean in Phoenix When You're Homeless

By Common Ladder · May 29, 2026 · Updated September 14, 2026 · 5 min read

If you need a shower, clean clothes, or hygiene supplies today, the fastest step is to call 211 (or 877-211-8661). A live 211 Arizona specialist can tell you which day resource centers are open right now, where the nearest shower is, and how to get there. Live operators answer 9 a.m.–7 p.m., seven days a week — outside those hours you reach an automated system.

This guide covers free places in Phoenix that offer showers, restrooms, laundry, clothing, and hygiene kits. Most are clustered downtown near the Key Campus (formerly the Human Services Campus), but there are options across the city. Hours change often — always confirm before you walk over.

Day Resource Centers (Showers + Restrooms)

These are the easiest places to find a shower during the day. Lines are shortest before 9 a.m.

Lodestar Day Resource Center

Address: Key Campus, 204 S 12th Ave, Phoenix, AZ 85007
Phone: 602-883-4509 (Brian Garcia Welcome Center, 206 S. 12th Ave. — call ahead) or 602-282-0853 (Key Campus main line, business hours)
Showers, restrooms, day storage, charging stations, ID assistance, and case management. The Key Campus (formerly the Human Services Campus, now run by Keys to Change) is the central hub for adult homeless services in Phoenix. Services are split across buildings: showers are in outdoor trailers, and mail is handled at the Brian Garcia Welcome Center, not at Lodestar.

Andre House of Arizona

Address: 213 S 11th Ave, Phoenix, AZ 85007
Phone: 602-255-0580
Showers, restrooms, hot meals, a clothing closet, and hygiene kits. Each service has its own window, and the line forms before the door opens.

  • Showers: Mon–Thu, 12:30–3:00 p.m. (line forms at noon). There are no Saturday showers.
  • Clothing closet: Tue & Thu 12:30–3:00 p.m. (line at noon); Sat 1:00–3:00 p.m. (line at 12:30).
  • Laundry: Mon–Wed 8:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m. Line up before 8:00 — there are only 16 slots, and pickup is 3:00 p.m. the same day.
  • Hygiene kits, backpacks, blankets: Mon–Thu and Sat–Sun, 1:00–3:00 p.m.
  • Dinner: Sat–Thu 5:00–6:00 p.m. (line forms at 4:00).

Brian Garcia Welcome Center

Located on the Key Campus at 206 S 12th Ave; phone 602-883-4509. The building is open 24 hours a day, and intake assessments run 7 a.m.–11 p.m. This is the intake point for adults seeking shelter or services in Maricopa County, and it is where campus mail is handled. Staff can connect you to showers, the Coordinated Entry list, ID recovery, and case management.

St. Vincent de Paul

Phoenix Dining Room (single adults): 1075 W Jackson St, Phoenix, AZ 85007, on the Key Campus.
Resource Center (showers, clothing, hygiene): 320 W Watkins Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85003 — 602-261-6883.
General information: call 211. St. Vincent de Paul has moved its site to svdpaz.org and no longer publishes a help line; the 602-266-HOPE number this page carried could not be confirmed as still answered (checked 2026-10-26)
Meals at the Phoenix Dining Room: breakfast Mon–Fri 7:00–8:00 a.m., lunch daily 11:00 a.m.–12:45 p.m., dinner Fri 4:00–5:00 p.m. Showers, clothing, and hygiene supplies are at the Resource Center on Watkins Rd, not at the dining room — call 602-261-6883 to confirm the day's hours before you walk over.

Note: the family dining room at 420 W Watkins Rd serves families with a child under 18 only. A single adult sent there for a meal will be turned away — go to 1075 W Jackson St instead.

UMOM New Day Centers (Families with Children)

Phone: 602-275-7852 (main line)
Serves families with children — emergency shelter, day services, case management. Showers and laundry are available for residents and during program intake. Families seeking shelter should call 2-1-1 first, then the Family Housing Hub at 602-595-8700 (Mon–Fri 8–5), which is the county's intake route for families with children. Single women without children: Halle Women's Center, 3424 E Van Buren St, 602-362-5833.

Free Clothing

You can usually get a basic outfit — pants, shirt, socks, underwear, and, when available, shoes — at one of the following:

  • Andre House Clothing Room — 213 S 11th Ave. Tue & Thu 12:30–3:00 p.m., Sat 1:00–3:00 p.m.
  • St. Vincent de Paul Resource Center — 320 W Watkins Rd, 602-261-6883. Clothing and hygiene supplies. This is a separate site from the dining room.
  • Phoenix Rescue Mission — 602-233-3000 (one line for all locations). Transforming Lives Center for Men, 1801 S 35th Ave; Changing Lives Center for Women & Children, 338 N 15th Ave. Clothing is generally for program participants; their Hope Coach street outreach distributes hygiene kits.
  • Salvation Army — Phoenix Citadel, 628 N 3rd Ave, 602-252-4478. Assistance services (food boxes, utility and rental help) run out of Phoenix Social Services, 2702 E Washington St, 602-267-4122, Mon–Fri 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Clothing is not a published program at either site — call before you go.
  • The Worker (formerly St. Joseph the Worker) — 382 E Palm Lane #150, Phoenix, AZ 85004, or 1833 W Main St #128, Mesa. Phone: 602-755-JOBS (602-755-5627). Interview clothing, uniforms, work tools, and transportation help for job seekers. They no longer have a Key Campus office, and they refer ID requests to the Homeless ID Project.
  • Goodwill Career Centers (Central & Northern Arizona) — 602-535-4444. Free resume help, mock interviews, and employer connections. They do not run a clothing program — for interview clothing, use The Worker above.

Laundry

Free laundry is harder to find than showers in Phoenix, but a few options exist:

  • Andre House — Mon–Wed 8:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m., 16 slots only. Line up before 8:00 a.m.; pickup is 3:00 p.m. the same day.
  • Lodestar Day Resource Center — washer/dryer access rotates by program; check at the front desk.
  • Shelter residents can typically use on-site laundry.
  • 211 referrals sometimes include laundry vouchers for participating providers.

If you can't access free laundry, a coin laundromat early in the morning is usually the cheapest paid option — wait times are shorter and machines are cleaner.

Hygiene Kits

If you don't have soap, a toothbrush, or other basics, ask at:

  • Andre House — kits, backpacks, and blankets Mon–Thu and Sat–Sun, 1:00–3:00 p.m.
  • Phoenix Rescue Mission — kits given during Hope Coach street outreach; 602-233-3000.
  • Maricopa County Human Services Department — occasional supply distributions; ask 211 for the current schedule.
  • Street outreach teams — multiple faith-based and nonprofit teams operate weekly walks in downtown Phoenix and hand out kits.

Cooling Centers (Summer — May 1 through September 30)

The Maricopa County Heat Relief Network runs May 1 through September 30. During the season, more than 200 cooling and hydration sites across the county offer water, restrooms, and sometimes showers. Locations and hours change year to year and site to site.

The 2026 season ends September 30. After that date most cooling sites close until May 2027, and 211 is the place to ask what stays open.

  • Heat Relief Map: hrn.azmag.gov — every site, with hours, services, and which ones are pet-friendly.
  • By phone: call 2-1-1 (or 877-211-8661). Live heat-relief specialists are on 9 a.m.–7 p.m. daily, and some callers qualify for a ride to and from a heat relief site.

These centers can be lifesaving when temperatures climb above 105 °F, which is most days in a Phoenix summer.

What to Bring (If You Can)

A small kit makes everything faster: a hand towel, soap or body wash, a toothbrush and toothpaste, deodorant, and one change of clothes in a plastic bag. If you don't have any of this, ask any of the providers above for a hygiene kit — most will hand one over without paperwork.

What to Expect

Day centers in Phoenix are busiest in the morning. Arrive before 9 a.m. for the shortest shower lines. Some sites ask you to sign in or show ID; if you don't have ID, say so. Most sites will still let you in and can help you start the ID recovery process. (See our guide: How to Replace a Lost ID in Phoenix When You're Experiencing Homelessness.)

If one site is full, closed for cleaning, or out of supplies, ask the staff which provider has space — most outreach workers and case managers know each other's hours and can route you somewhere open today.

Always verify hours, addresses, and phone numbers by calling 211 before you go. Resources in Phoenix change frequently. Every provider on this page was re-verified against the provider's own website on September 14, 2026, but hours drift between checks — 211 Arizona is the most current real-time source.

Have a correction or an update? Contact us — we keep this information current.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take a free shower in Phoenix?

Day resource centers downtown offer free showers, including the Key Campus at 204 S 12th Ave and Andre House at 213 S 11th Ave. Andre House showers run Monday through Thursday, 12:30 to 3:00 p.m., with the line forming at noon.

What if I don't have ID for a shower?

Some sites ask you to sign in or show ID, but if you don't have one, say so. Most sites will still let you in and can help you start the ID recovery process.

Where can I get free clothes in Phoenix?

You can usually get a basic outfit at the Andre House Clothing Room (213 S 11th Ave, Tue and Thu 12:30 to 3:00 p.m., Sat 1:00 to 3:00 p.m.) or the St. Vincent de Paul Resource Center (320 W Watkins Rd, 602-261-6883). For interview and work clothing, The Worker, formerly St. Joseph the Worker, is at 382 E Palm Lane #150, 602-755-5627.

How do I get a hygiene kit if I have no supplies?

Ask at Andre House, the Phoenix Rescue Mission, or a street outreach team. Most providers will hand over a hygiene kit without paperwork.

How can I find out which centers are open right now?

Call or text 211 (or 877-211-8661). The 211 Arizona operator can tell you which day resource centers are open, where the nearest shower is, and how to get there.
