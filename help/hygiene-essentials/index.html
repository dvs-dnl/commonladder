Hygiene & Essentials

Hygiene & Essentials resources near you

Showers, laundry, clothing, hygiene supplies, and basic essential items — available at no cost at sites across the metro area.

How to use this directory

Day shelters and community organizations across the Phoenix area offer free showers, laundry, and hygiene supplies, usually on a first-come, first-served basis. Bring whatever ID you have, but most sites will serve you without it. Clothing is typically available in limited sizes — call ahead when possible.
