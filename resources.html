Emergency Shelter

Human Services Campus (CASS)

Single Adults

Arizona's largest emergency shelter campus. Walk-in intake. Coordinated Entry access on-site. Includes CASS, Lodestar Day Resource Center, Andre House, and 15+ partner organizations.

1132 S. 12th Ave, Phoenix, AZ 85034 (602) 256-6945 Walk-in; call for current hours

UMOM New Day Centers

Families

What to do: Families: call the Family Housing Hub, 602-595-8700. Single women: call the Halle Women's Center, 602-362-5833. UMOM does not serve single men. For: Families with children (through the Family Housing Hub); single women without children (Halle Women's Center)

3333 E. Van Buren St., Phoenix, AZ 85008 (602) 275-7852 — UMOM main line (not intake) umom.org

St. Vincent de Paul — Phoenix Dining Room

Meals

What to do: Walk in at meal times. Overnight beds are available here year-round — call to ask whether any are open. For: Anyone experiencing homelessness

1075 W. Jackson St., Phoenix, AZ 85007 (602) 478-8172 — Ask about open overnight beds Breakfast: Mon–Fri 7–8 a.m. · Lunch: daily 11 a.m.–12:45 p.m. · Dinner: Fridays only, 4–5 p.m. · Heat relief: June 1–Sept. 30, daily 1:30–5 p.m. svdpaz.org

Families with kids

What to do: Walk in during service hours. For: Families with a child under 18 — open to anyone with a child under 18. Single adults are served at the Phoenix Dining Room instead.

420 W. Watkins Rd., Phoenix, AZ 85003 Mon–Fri 4–6 p.m. svdpaz.org

Andre House — meals, showers, laundry, clothing

Hot dinner

What to do: Walk in. Lines form before each service. Andre House is not a shelter and does not offer housing. For: Anyone who comes to the door

213 S. 11th Ave., Phoenix, AZ 85007 (602) 255-0580 Dinner: Sat–Thu 5–6 p.m. (line forms at 4 p.m.) — no dinner on Fridays · Showers: Mon–Thu 12:30–3 p.m. (line forms at noon) · Laundry: Mon–Wed 8:30 a.m.–noon (16 slots; line forms before 8 a.m.; pick up at 3 p.m.) · Clothing closet: Tue and Thu 12:30–3 p.m.; Sat 1–3 p.m. · Hygiene kits, backpacks, blankets: Mon–Thu and Sat–Sun 1–3 p.m. andrehouse.org

Health & Medical Care

Circle the City

Free Primary Care

The largest provider of healthcare for people experiencing homelessness in Arizona. Primary care, chronic disease management, wound care, and pharmacy — at no cost to unhoused patients. Walk-ins accepted, no insurance required. Medical respite program available for post-hospital recovery.

1001 N. Central Ave., Phoenix, AZ 85004 (602) 441-5000 Mon–Fri 8:00 AM–5:00 PM circlethecity.org

Housing Programs & Case Management

Coordinated Entry System — Maricopa County

Housing Access

The required pathway for accessing HUD-funded housing programs — Rapid Rehousing and Permanent Supportive Housing — in Maricopa County. Clients are assessed and matched based on need. Access through HSC, UMOM, and other designated sites. Ask a case manager or call 211 to get started.

Start by calling 211 maricoparegionalcoc.org

Housing Assistance

Short-term rental assistance and services to help people move quickly out of shelter into stable housing. Multiple providers in Maricopa County. Eligibility and availability vary. Access through Coordinated Entry.

Youth Services (Under 25)

HomeBase Youth Services (Native American Connections)

Ages 18–24

Primary provider for young adults experiencing homelessness in Phoenix. Dorm-style emergency shelter, drop-in center (M–F 7am–4pm) with meals, laundry, showers, and case management, plus transitional living and rapid rehousing. A Youth Coordinated Entry Access Point — standard CE processes are often inappropriate for youth, so HomeBase operates the youth-specific pathway.

(602) 263-5531 nativeconnections.org
